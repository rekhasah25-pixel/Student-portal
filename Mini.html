Magi Recipe 😋🍜

Maggi is a quick and testy noodle dish. It is very to make and loved by everyone.

  1. 1 packet maggi noodles
  2. 1 cup water
  3. Maggi tastemaker

Instruction

  1. Boil water in a pan.
  2. Add Maggi noodles.
  3. Add tastemaker.
  4. Cook for 2 minutes.

Note:Do not overcook the noodles.

serve hot and enjoy!
